# **BriefFlow**  
  
**Case Study**  
  
AI tool that converts messy client inputs into structured, client-approved creative briefs in minutes to cut revision cycles and budget waste.  
  
## **Project Details**  
  
**Role:** Partner, AI Product Designer, Automation Specialist  
  
**Focus:** AI-powered briefing, client workflow optimization, creative process automation  
  
**Built With:** Claude Code + Codex CLI + Gemini + Traycer.ai, Next.js, Supabase  
  
##  **Overview**

### **Problem**  
Creative teams lose weeks to unclear client input and weak briefing processes. On average, 21–56 days vanish in review loops while 33% of marketing budgets are wasted on rework and misaligned deliverables.

### **Challenge** 
Traditional briefing depends on manual intake, back-and-forth revisions, and scattered communication. Teams burn time translating client notes into structure, while project scopes drift and accountability disappears.

### **Solution** 
BriefFlow uses AI orchestration to compress that chaos into clarity. It captures the right inputs, generates structured creative briefs in minutes, and delivers client-approved outputs with audit trails—turning weeks of friction into a single, efficient workflow.
  
### **Why We Built BriefFlow**  
  
Years in agency environments showed how dysfunctional briefing kills momentum. Teams spend 21–56 days in review loops while client trust and internal bandwidth erode. We built BriefFlow to compress that entire cycle—giving teams time back for growth, strategy, and relationship-building. 
  
### **Briefing Process Hell**  
Creative teams waste nearly one-third of project time on poor briefing habits and chaotic client inputs that lead to endless revisions.  
  
### **Budget Waste**  
External agencies and poor briefing processes cost companies millions in wasted marketing spend and failed projects.  
  
### **Client Communication Gaps**  
Teams struggle with unstructured client requirements, leading to scope creep and deliverables that miss the mark.

## **AI Agent Orchestration**  
  
### **How I built BriefFlow using multi-agent coordination**  
  
From identifying marketing briefing pain points to orchestrating AI tools to build a production-ready platform.  
  
### **01 - Marketing Research**  
We reached out to several of our old colleagues regarding this issue we had a hunch was still present in the advertising agency world. It surely was. We met with 12 of our ex colleagues in the product mgmt and account mgmt space.  
  
- **Research Tool:** Open-ended zoom chats  
- **Focus:** Confirming creative brief inefficiencies and client communication gaps  
  
### **02 - AI Architecture Design**  
I leveraged Gemini and Claude mostly to design the AI briefing system, create a design system, and focus on top notch UX/UI experiences  
  
- **Architecture Agent:** Claude Code & Gemini provide a great solid planning base prior to implementation, and Claude specifically, does an amazing job of providing users with the best AI engineering UX/UI practices  
- **Output:** Smart intake forms and approval workflows  
  
### **03 - Implementation**  
I used **Codex CLI** to build rapid implementation of form builders, AI processing pipelines, and integration systems that transform chaotic inputs into structured briefs.  
  
- **Implementation Agent:** Codex CLI for workflow automation and backend reviews. Codex has such a sharp eye for backend implementation and efficiencies. It is able to remove a ton of fluff to enhance user and developer experiences alike through codebase optimizations  
- **Result:** Minutes instead of days for brief creation  
  
### **04 - Production Platform**  
The orchestrated AI agents delivered a platform that saves agencies 30% of marketing budget by eliminating briefing waste and revision cycles.  
  
- **Achievement:** 30% reduction in briefing time and budget waste  
- **Impact:** Structured client communication at scale, and internal efficiencies in brief approvals
