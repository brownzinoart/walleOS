## EARLY CAREER: NYC AGENCIES (ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT FOUNDATIONS)
I started my career in New York City advertising agencies, spending the first five years on the account management side. I led teams that delivered award‑winning campaigns and impact‑driven consumer solutions. My first major role was as an Account Coordinator at RosettaWishbone, supporting Pfizer’s Prevnar 13 Adult Vaccines global initiative. One of my earliest accomplishments was organizing and executing a KOL conference in Dubai. Since I spoke Arabic, I managed everything from travel logistics to stipend distribution directly with KOLs and ensured on‑the‑ground execution ran flawlessly. It was a formative experience—equal parts cultural diplomacy, logistics, and leadership—wrapped in my first global client trip.

Account management in NYC was my professional boot camp. I consistently worked on flagship accounts at every agency. At FCB Health, for example, I managed the consumer side of a $10MM account for NUVIGIL, overseeing four creative teams, a strategy unit, and full production across radio, print, and digital. Those years gave me access to the decision‑making table early, teaching me how to strategize across creative, production, and client layers. That confidence and cross‑functional fluency became the foundation for my later transition into UX.

---

## TRANSITION: FROM ADVERTISING TO UX
After half a decade of New York’s intensity, I relocated to San Diego and joined Scout Marketing, where I could explore user experience design. I became a bridge between account management and UX, mentoring under the new SVP of UX and helping the agency adopt user‑centered design thinking. I leaned on client communication and translation skills—turning complex goals into clear tasks for creative, strategy, and tech teams. That phase accelerated my pivot into UX. I freelanced in NYC, then joined Heartbeat Ideas full‑time before moving on to Kinesso.

---

## KINESSO: SYSTEMS THINKING AND TEAM BUILDING
I joined as the second member of a nascent design team tasked with transforming developer‑built analytics tools into consumer‑ready SaaS products. We built everything from scratch and scaled from two to thirty designers across global time zones during COVID. We shipped award‑winning systems (DXA, Splash), implemented UX governance, and built a team that enjoyed the work. Under IPG, Kinesso felt like a startup with enterprise resources—energizing and effective.

---

## NOW: BUILDING AI SYSTEMS WITH PURPOSE
Today I focus on AI and orchestration through my company, One Block Away LLC. I treat LLMs as collaborators—apply design‑thinking to AI workflows: research, plan, implement, test, repeat. I specialize in model selection, orchestration, and agentic workflows using Codex, Claude, Gemini, GLM 4.6, Cursor, and more. My goal: give teams efficiency and creative bandwidth with responsible, usable systems.

## VOICE & TONE REFERENCE
I write with precision and directness. Every sentence must earn its place. I avoid filler and emotional exaggeration but still convey conviction. My tone balances system‑level reasoning with hands‑on practicality. Lead with impact, then process, then proof. Treat design, leadership, and AI implementation as one discipline—clarity, scalability, and function.
