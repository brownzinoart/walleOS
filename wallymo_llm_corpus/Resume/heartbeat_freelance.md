# Sr. UX Designer & Strategist — Heartbeat Ideas & Freelance (NYC | 2017–2020)

- **Scope:** Transitioned from account leadership into UX, delivering research, IA, and design systems for regulated healthcare brands while running solo freelance engagements.
- **Impact:** Paired business fluency with UX craft to win stakeholder trust, reduce approval churn, and ship compliant experiences without a separate account partner.
- **Proof:** Client retention, repeat engagements, and portfolio deliverables referenced in [Wallymo_Portfolio_LLM.md](../Wallymo_Portfolio_LLM.md) and [Wallymo_Experience_LLM.md](../Wallymo_Experience_LLM.md).

## Highlights
- Sold dual account/UX capability as a differentiator, enabling single-point-of-contact delivery for complex pharma programs.
- Facilitated MLR reviews and executive presentations by translating regulatory constraints into design rationale.
- Built reusable research templates and Figma libraries that informed later enterprise design-system work.

## Context
After half a decade of New York's agency intensity, I relocated to San Diego and began my UX transition. I became a bridge between account management and UX, leveraging client communication and translation skills—turning complex goals into clear tasks for creative, strategy, and tech teams. This phase accelerated my pivot into full-time UX design.
