# FCB Health: The Growth Platform That Defined Professional Excellence

## Overview

Wally Mostafa's tenure at FCB Health (April 2011 - May 2015) represents one of the most significant periods of growth and achievement in his career. Spanning four years and progressing from Account Coordinator to Sr. Account Executive, this role transformed him from a promising newcomer into a trusted leader managing one of the agency's most prestigious accounts. The FCB Health experience built directly upon the foundation forged at RosettaWishbone, elevating his capabilities from handling pressure to creating opportunities, from translating languages to shaping strategy.

## Rapid Growth and Increasing Autonomy

### From Account Coordinator to Sr. Account Executive

Wally's progression at FCB Health was marked by consistent advancement and expanding responsibilities:

> "I went from Account Coordinator to Account Executive to Sr. Account Executive in about three years. Each promotion came with more autonomy, more trust, and more responsibility. They weren't just giving me a new title—they were giving me real ownership."

This rapid advancement reflected both his capability and the agency's culture of recognizing and rewarding performance. The autonomy he was granted was exceptional for someone at his career level:

- **Decision-making authority** on account strategy
- **Direct client relationships** without constant supervision
- **Budget responsibility** for significant campaign investments
- **Team leadership** opportunities across multiple projects

### The Trust Factor

What distinguished FCB Health was the level of trust they placed in young talent:

> "FCB Health was amazing at giving people rope. If you proved you could handle responsibility, they gave you more. It wasn't about years of experience—it was about results and judgment."

This trust-based culture enabled Wally to take on challenges that would typically be reserved for more senior professionals, accelerating his development exponentially.

## The NUVIGIL Account: A $10M Responsibility

### One of Two Account Managers

The NUVIGIL account for Teva Pharmaceuticals represented a significant business opportunity—valued at approximately $10 million—and Wally was entrusted as one of only two account managers managing this critical relationship:

> "Being one of only two account managers on a $10M account was incredible. There was nowhere to hide. Every decision mattered, every client interaction was high-stakes, and every campaign had to deliver results."

This responsibility taught him several crucial lessons:

- **Strategic thinking** at the enterprise level
- **Client relationship management** with senior pharmaceutical executives
- **Budget management** and resource allocation
- **Risk assessment** and mitigation strategies

### Beyond SOW Execution: Strategic Partnership

What made the NUVIGIL experience transformative was how Wally expanded the account manager role beyond traditional Statement of Work (SOW) execution:

> "I realized quickly that being a good account manager wasn't just about delivering what was in the SOW. It was about understanding the client's business challenges, anticipating their needs, and bringing ideas that helped them achieve their broader goals."

This strategic approach included:
- **Brand planning** beyond individual campaigns
- **Market analysis** and competitive intelligence
- **Business development** through proactive problem-solving
- **Long-term relationship building** with multiple stakeholders

## Groundbreaking Achievements and Industry Firsts

### First Pharma Brand on Xbox Live

One of the most innovative achievements during Wally's tenure was making NUVIGIL the first pharmaceutical brand to advertise on Xbox Live—a groundbreaking move that required navigating uncharted regulatory territory:

> "Getting a pharma brand on Xbox Live was insane. No one had done it before. We had to figure out how to satisfy FDA requirements while creating an experience that actually worked for the gaming audience. It took months of negotiation with MLR, legal teams, and Microsoft, but we made it happen."

This achievement demonstrated:
- **Regulatory innovation** and creative problem-solving
- **Cross-platform thinking** and audience expansion
- **Risk management** in unregulated territories
- **Collaboration skills** across multiple stakeholders

### Times Square Digital Domination

Another landmark achievement was securing Times Square digital screen placements for NUVIGIL, creating unprecedented visibility for the brand:

> "Seeing our campaign on those massive Times Square screens was surreal. We weren't just running digital ads—we were part of the NYC landscape. It required incredible coordination, budget management, and client trust, but the impact was massive."

These high-visibility successes established Wally's reputation for delivering innovative solutions that pushed boundaries while maintaining compliance.

## Building Strong MLR Relationships and Finding Compromises

### The MLR Challenge: Regulatory Gatekeepers

Working in pharmaceutical advertising means constant interaction with Medical, Legal, and Regulatory (MLR) teams—the gatekeepers who ensure all communications comply with FDA regulations. Many account managers view MLR as obstacles, but Wally took a different approach:

> "I learned early that MLR teams weren't the enemy—they were partners in finding compliant ways to innovate. Instead of fighting them, I built relationships. I understood their concerns, spoke their language, and worked with them to find solutions."

### The Art of Compromise and Innovation

This collaborative approach enabled several breakthrough innovations:

- **Creative solutions** that satisfied regulatory requirements
- **Educational approaches** that helped MLR understand new platforms
- **Compromise frameworks** that balanced innovation with compliance
- **Trust building** that accelerated approval processes

> "The best innovations came from compromise. We'd bring something crazy, MLR would explain why it wouldn't work, and instead of giving up, we'd iterate together until we found something that was both innovative and compliant."

## Managing 25+ Team Members: Leadership at Scale

### The Hot Sheet Coordination System

As campaigns grew in complexity, Wally developed and implemented an effective coordination system using "hot sheets" to manage teams of 25+ people across multiple disciplines:

> "Managing 25+ people without a system is chaos. I created these hot sheets that were essentially daily coordination documents. Everyone knew what they were responsible for, who they needed to coordinate with, and what the deadlines were. It kept massive teams moving in the same direction."

The hot sheet system included:
- **Daily responsibility matrices** for all team members
- **Interdependency tracking** between departments
- **Deadline management** and milestone tracking
- **Communication protocols** for rapid decision-making

### Cross-Functional Leadership

This experience taught him valuable lessons about large-scale team management:

- **Clear communication** is more important than detailed management
- **Empowerment** creates better results than micromanagement
- **Coordination systems** scale better than individual relationships
- **Visual project tracking** keeps everyone aligned
