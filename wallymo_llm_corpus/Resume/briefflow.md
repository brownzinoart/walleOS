# BriefFlow — AI-Powered Creative Brief Automation

**Product URL:** https://briefflow.app/
**Partnership:** Pragmatic Digital (co-design collaboration)

## Overview
BriefFlow is an AI-powered platform that streamlines creative brief generation for marketing agencies, transforming unstructured client inputs into professionally formatted, client-approved creative briefs in minutes rather than days.

## Problem
Marketing agencies waste approximately one-third of project time on poor briefing and revision cycles. Typical review periods span 21-56 days, creating bottlenecks that delay project kickoff, erode budgets, and frustrate both agency teams and clients. Unstructured inputs, missing information, and endless revision loops compound these inefficiencies.

## Solution
BriefFlow transforms brief creation through a three-stage intelligent workflow:

### 1. Smart Intake Forms
Conditional logic guides clients through relevant questions while capturing necessary information. Includes voice recording submission capability for clients who prefer verbal communication over written forms.

### 2. AI Brief Generation
Advanced AI analyzes inputs and produces structured briefs following agency templates and brand guidelines. Automatically identifies missing information and prompts for clarification before generating final output.

### 3. Client Approval Workflow
Branded review links enable seamless client commenting, feedback, and sign-off. Eliminates email chains and version control issues by centralizing collaboration in a single interface.

### 4. Export & Integration
Supports PDF export for archival and presentation. Direct integration with project management tools ensures approved briefs flow immediately into execution workflows.
