# Account Leadership — FCB Health & Scout Marketing (NYC & San Diego | 2014–2017)

- **Scope:** Led national pharmaceutical campaigns, spanning broadcast production for Teva’s Linzess and omnichannel programs for Jazz Pharma’s Xyrem while mentoring cross-functional agency teams.
- **Impact:** Shortened FDA/network clearance loops, orchestrated three TV shoots under tight timelines, and delivered program engagement lifts that paved the way for a UX transition.
- **Proof:** Stakeholder testimonials and campaign performance data referenced in [Wallymo_Experience_LLM.md](../Wallymo_Experience_LLM.md); production rigor documented through Linzess launch retrospectives.

- Coordinated end-to-end TV production—including casting, budget control, and network delivery—across three Linzess spots without schedule slips.
