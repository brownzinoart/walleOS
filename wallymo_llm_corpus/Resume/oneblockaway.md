## ListingPal Snapshot
**Problem (128 words):** Real-estate agents juggle 3–5 disconnected tools, slow manual copywriting, and compliance reviews that stretch listings over several hours. Marketing deliverables often require agency help, and the resulting assets lack MLS-friendly language, SEO rigor, and tailored social hooks. Smaller teams lose momentum because every listing needs fresh descriptions, ad variants, and interior styling suggestions, which also makes A/B testing expensive. Beta interviews in North Carolina confirmed that even tech-forward brokerages rely on spreadsheets and freelance writers to glue the process together. The friction compounds when agents need to respond to market shifts quickly, meaning they settle for generic copy. ListingPal targets these pain points by replacing manual stitching with a predictable 90-second workflow.

**Solution (133 words):** ListingPal wraps the proprietary AgentSelect™ orchestrator around a React/Tailwind front end and Node-based service layer. Agents drop in an address and price; the system enriches context through MLS and public-record lookups, then parallelizes LLM prompts tuned for MLS compliance, SEO density, and social cadence. AgentSelect routes tasks to the best available model, keeping tone consistent across long-form listings, Instagram/Facebook captions, ad headlines, and even interior styling prompts. Continuous model refresh keeps outputs sharp without manual updates. Calendly and MailerLite integrations nudge conversions into consults or onboarding. The entire package exports in platform-ready formats, so agents walk away with descriptions, paid media copy, and styling notes in under 90 seconds, without managing prompts or vendor contracts.

**Evidence (118 words):** Beta agents report collapsing copywriting time from hours to 90 seconds, citing consistent MLS acceptance on the first submission. The orchestrator consolidates three to five legacy tools, reducing monthly software spend while adding high-CTR ad variants for retargeting. Early usage logs show parallel runs staying within cost guardrails by selecting lighter models when copy tone tolerates it, while routing ad creative to premium models where ROI payoff is higher. The NC cohort continues to expand through referrals, and onboarding conversations feed a prioritized feature queue. These findings flow back into One Block Away’s shared architecture, letting WeReady inherit orchestration patterns and keeping all products aligned on speed, compliance, and measurable impact.
