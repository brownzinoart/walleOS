# Echo — Pharmaceutical Screenshot Automation & QA Platform

**Product URL:** https://echo.hedgehox.com
**Partnership:** GTM support and sales choreography alongside former colleague (Hedgehox)

## Overview
Echo is a SaaS platform that automates screenshot capture and accelerates route preparation and quality assurance for pharmaceutical marketing teams. Eliminates manual workflows to deliver "submission-ready screenshots, in minutes."

## Problem
Pharmaceutical agencies and marketing teams face tight submission timelines with complex digital route preparation workflows. Manual screenshot capture across multiple devices, viewports, and environments is time-intensive, error-prone, and creates QA bottlenecks. Traditional methods require 8+ hours per route with inconsistent quality and high risk of human error.

## Solution
Cloud-based screenshot automation platform built by industry veterans with 25+ years combined pharmaceutical agency experience. Processes screenshots server-side with no local resource requirements, ensuring compatibility with enterprise IT and security protocols.

## Key Features

### Lightning-Fast Capture
Processes 100 screenshots in under 5 minutes across desktop, tablet, and mobile formats with automatic padding and framing.

### Environment Management
Consolidates staging and production environments in one interface with customizable breakpoints, margins, and credentials per project.

### Conditional Logic System
Applies project-wide IF/THEN actions like element repositioning, section expansion, and strategic delays based on specific pages, viewports, and states.

### Page-Level Customization
Enables reordering, adding, or removing pages with granular control over clicks, hovers, delays, and other interactions without code.

### QA Integration
Supports testing form logic, error states, and dynamic content verification including personalized elements and interactive calculators.

## Technical Architecture
- Cloud-based SaaS platform (not a browser extension)
- Server-side screenshot processing with no local resource requirements
- Secure infrastructure compatible with enterprise IT and security protocols
- Multi-environment credential management
- Automated viewport and device simulation
