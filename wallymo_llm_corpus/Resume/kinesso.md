# Wally Mostafa - Director, Experience & Innovation at Kinesso (IPG)

His leadership established user experience as a cornerstone discipline, created award-winning products and systems, and positioned Kinesso at the forefront of innovation in marketing intelligence technology.

**Key Achievement:** Built and led a 30-person international design team, growing from the second hire in the design organization to a global leader in UX innovation and strategy.

## User Research Foundation

### Creating UX Discipline from Scratch

As the 2nd hire on the design team, Wally faced the challenge of establishing user research as a fundamental discipline within Kinesso's extensive Marketing Intelligence Engine (MIE) ecosystem of 20+ interconnected products. The existing approach to research was inconsistent and often treated as a universal tool regardless of product or audience needs.

### Strategic Research Roadshow

Wally embarked on a comprehensive roadshow across the MIE suite, forging close partnerships with product and development leads across four key verticals:
- **Audiences Suite**
- **Activation Suite** 
- **Optimization Suite**
- **Reporting Suite**

This initiative established a shared understanding and language around design thinking education and strategy, positioning research at the heart of the design-thinking process.

### Tools and Documentation Implementation

Wally introduced and implemented a comprehensive research toolkit:

**Research Tools:**
- **Typeform:** Real-time feedback capture and swift analysis
- **Useberry:** Interactive prototype testing with actionable insights from user flows
- **Heap:** Powerful analytics for measuring user interactions in real-time
- **Microsoft Teams:** Revolutionized interview documentation with real-time transcription and recording capabilities

**Figma Documentation System:**
- **Wireframe Kit:** Tailored to the design system, spanning low to high fidelity for accelerated mockup creation
- **Information Architecture:** Clear sitemaps, user journeys, and 50 personas for consistent understanding across teams
- **Flexible Scripts:** Adaptable research scripts for diverse conversations and user-centric feedback
- **Findings Deck:** Standardized template promoting uniformity with deep-linking capabilities to prototypes

### White Glove User Experience Approach

Wally implemented an exceptional user experience framework that valued and elevated user contributions:

- **Consistent Communication:** Regular updates to research participants on project statuses
- **Gratitude in Action:** Personalized thank-you emails acknowledging unique contributions
- **Open-Access Knowledge:** Comprehensive research library in Figma accessible to all employees
- **Beta Group Engagements:** Dedicated beta groups for real-time feedback and agile refinements

### Research Process Framework

Wally established a consistent yet flexible four-stage research process:

1. **The List:** Comprehensive user selection encompassing super-users, novices, and newcomers with regional diversity
2. **Scheduling:** Meticulous 30-60 minute interviews with minimum three-day notice and clear objective communication
3. **Research Time:** Recorded sessions via Teams with genuine engagement to build empathy across the user base
4. **Findings Report:** Standardized reporting system using templatized approaches for clarity and stakeholder efficiency

### Transformative Impact

**Regional-Informed Decisions:** The research framework illuminated distinct regional variations in user behavior, enabling tailored user experiences across different markets.

**Research Team Creation:** As research became a project cornerstone, the team expanded dramatically from 2 to 30 international designers in 3.5 years, necessitating dedicated research team hiring.

**Design Research Internships:** The success led to internal internships where team members from marketing, learning, and data science departments interned with the design team, spreading research ethos across the organization.

## Splash Design System

### Award-Winning Design System Achievement

Wally led the creation of the award-winning Splash Design System, collaborating with three UI designers to craft a comprehensive system that earned **2023 Indigo Awards: 3x Gold (UX Interface & Navigation, Digital Tools & Utilities, Graphic Design) and 2x Silver (Interaction and Interactive Design)**.

### System Scale and Scope

The Splash Design System represents a monumental achievement in design system architecture:

- **500 Components:** Comprehensive library covering all interaction patterns
- **140 Styles:** Complete design language including typography, colors, spacing, and more
- **300 Icons:** Extensive iconography system for consistent visual communication
- **50 Personas:** Detailed user personas guiding design decisions across products

### Research-Driven Approach

In a developer-led environment where user needs were often overlooked, Wally championed a research-driven approach. During the initial two months while UI designers shaped the system's aesthetics, Wally conducted interviews with teams to identify priorities and feasible rollout strategies.

### Key Innovations and Solutions

#### System-Wide Navigation
**Research Finding:** Users felt overwhelmed by navigation, often seeing products they didn't use when most only required 3-4 relevant products.

**Design Impact:** Created personalized navigation showing only relevant products with customization options, streamlining user journeys and adding personalization layers.

#### Global Color Picker
**Research Finding:** Users frequently exported data visualizations to adjust colors externally for client branding requirements.

**Design Impact:** Introduced dynamic customization allowing users to modify entire data page color schemes within the platform, with options to save and preset brand/client colors.

#### Design Bot Excellence
**Research Finding:** Challenge maintaining consistent implementation across expanding teams and products.

**Design Impact:** Developed proprietary "Design Bot" Chrome extension that audits designs for adherence to components and templates, generating comprehensive reports with corrective solutions and quantifying design precision.

#### Personalization Framework
**Research Finding:** Users desired tailored interactions with data viewing and dashboard processing.

**Design Impact:** Prioritized user personalization with customizable profile experiences and productivity features for beta users, enhancing user satisfaction.

#### White Label Automation
**Research Finding:** Growing client demand for ecosystem alignment with in-house branding elements.

**Design Impact:** Created "White Label Automation Builder" providing automated sequential process for custom client looks while maintaining core design system pillars and UX/UI standards.

### Measurable Business Impact

The Splash Design System delivered exceptional results across key metrics:

- **+68% Average Users Year-over-Year**
- **~7,000+ Average Monthly Users**
- **+79% Average Usage Rate Year-over-Year**

These metrics directly reflect the impact of the design system's implementation on user engagement and product adoption across the MIE suite.

### Living Documentation Philosophy

Recognizing the evolving nature of technology and design, Splash was conceived as a living document that grows, adapts, and evolves based on user feedback, industry trends, and technological advancements.
