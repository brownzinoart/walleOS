# Director, Experience & Innovation — Kinesso (IPG) (Remote | 2020–2023)

- **Scope:** Second hire on the design team; chartered to modernize 20+ analytics products, stand up UX research, and build a reusable design system for the Marketing Intelligence Engine.
- **Impact:** Systemized research ops, launched the Splash design system and DXA flagship product, and scaled the global design organization; metrics live in [Team Growth](../Wallymo_Metrics_and_Awards.md#team-growth) and [Product Metrics](../Wallymo_Metrics_and_Awards.md#product-metrics).
- **Proof:** Red Dot and Indigo awards validate the design outcomes; product adoption and reuse rates are documented in [Product Metrics](../Wallymo_Metrics_and_Awards.md#product-metrics).

## Highlights
- Established a four-stage research framework (“List → Schedule → Research → Findings”) adopted across 20+ product squads.
- Partnered with product and engineering leads to co-create Splash’s governance, personalization framework, and Design Bot auditing workflow.
- Led a multi-region team through pandemic-era scaling, pairing culture building with measurable delivery cadence.

## Context
I joined as the second member of a nascent design team tasked with transforming developer-built analytics tools into consumer-ready SaaS products. We built everything from scratch and scaled from two to thirty designers across global time zones during COVID. Under IPG, Kinesso felt like a startup with enterprise resources—energizing and effective.
