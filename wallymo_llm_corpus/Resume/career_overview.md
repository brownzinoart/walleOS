## Career Journey

### Phase 1: Pharmaceutical Account Management (2010-2017)

**Foundation in Client Relationships and Strategic Campaigns**

Wally began his career in pharmaceutical advertising, building a strong foundation in account management and client strategy:

- **Account Coordinator at RosettaWishbone - Pfizer Prevnar 13** (2010-2011): Coordinated KOL workshops in Dubai with Pfizer stakeholders, establishing early international collaboration skills
- **Account Executive to Sr. Account Executive at FCB Health** (2011-2015): Led unbranded campaigns and digital platform rebranding for Teva Pharmaceuticals' NUVIGIL, then supervised end-to-end production of three brand commercials for Linzess, coordinating FDA preclearance and network approvals
- **Account Supervisor at Scout Marketing** (2015-2017): Optimized key account campaigns for Jazz Pharmaceuticals' Xyrem, leveraging digital insights and leading unbranded disease awareness initiatives that boosted consumer engagement

*Key Learning:* "I learned how to manage complex stakeholder relationships and navigate regulatory environments while delivering measurable business results."

### Phase 2: Transition to UX Design (2017-2020)

**Pivoting to User-Centered Design**

Recognizing the growing importance of digital experiences, Wally strategically transitioned into UX design:

- **Freelance UX Designer for Pharma Ad Agencies** (2017-2018): Applied account management insights to user experience design
- **Sr. UX Designer at Heartbeat Ideas** (2018-2019): Managed comprehensive UX initiatives from research and wireframing to design systems, improving client satisfaction and project ROI across agency clients
- **Sr. Freelance UX Design** (2019-2020): Continued building UX expertise while maintaining client relationships

*Motivation:* "I saw that the best campaigns were failing because of poor user experiences. I wanted to be the person who could bridge strategy and execution."

### Phase 3: Leadership and Innovation (2020-2023)

**Scaling Design Organizations and Enterprise Transformation**

As Director of Experience & Innovation at Kinesso, Wally demonstrated exceptional leadership capabilities:

*Leadership Philosophy:* "Build people first, systems second. When you empower your team with the right frameworks and trust, they'll deliver beyond expectations."
