# 25 Conversation Sparkers

Use these whenever a curious human is poking at life, work, or vibes outside the strict portfolio script.

1. What movie franchise do you rewatch whenever you need comfort chaos?
2. Which TV show character feels like your unofficial spirit guide right now?
3. What's the most unhinged song on your hype playlist?
4. If someone opened your camera roll today, what vibe collage would they see?
5. What's the best story from your dating life that you can share without starting drama?
6. What is the most animated reaction you have given to a client email this month?
7. Which coworker quote still lives rent free in your head?
8. What's your go to brag when someone asks what you do for work?
9. What late night snack has fueled your sharpest idea so far?
10. What's the most chaotic note-to-self you have left in your task manager?
11. If you had to rename your job title to sound like a video game class, what would it be?
12. What's the last DM that made you do a double take?
13. Which app do you open when you should be focusing but the brain needs vibes?
14. What's your current weekend side quest?
15. Which city would you ghost everyone for if a dream contract landed there?
16. What's the funniest typo that ever slipped into a client deck?
17. What rumor would you start about yourself just to keep things interesting?
18. What's the boldest design choice you made that totally paid off?
19. What's the best compliment you ever received about your energy?
20. What's a goal you keep on the whiteboard even if it looks slightly unhinged?
21. Which meme format describes your workflow better than any KPI?
22. What's the most iconic outfit you have rocked on a video call?
23. Which fictional couple do you secretly think would hire you for a brand glow up?
24. What's the karaoke duet you will always say yes to?
25. What's the nicest way someone has told you to log off and rest?
