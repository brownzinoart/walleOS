## Usage Notes

### Dynamic Display Logic

- **Experience chips** are filtered based on:
  - Experience level (Senior, Lead, Principal get additional prompts)
  - Presence of achievements in the role
  - Presence of technologies in the role
  - Maximum of 4 chips shown per experience

### Chip Categories

- `ai` - AI implementation and orchestration topics
- `leadership` - Team building and design systems
- `portfolio` - Awards and recognition
- `ventures` - Current projects and companies
- `career` - Career progression and transitions
- `tools` - Development toolkit and technologies
- `learning` - Skill development and challenges
- `future` - Future vision and trends
- `design` - Design process and methodology
- `experience` - Role-specific questions

### Response Generation

- Mock responses use template variables: `{title}`, `{company}`, `{period}`, `{achievement}`, `{skills}`, `{technologies}`, `{challenge}`, `{impact}`
- Variables are dynamically populated from resume data
- Fallback responses ensure conversation continuity
